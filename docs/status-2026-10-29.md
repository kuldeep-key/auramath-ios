# Auramath mobile app: status

29 Oct 2026 · branch `feat/app-base` (not pushed; merges to `main` after your review) · Flutter, iPhone first, Android also working.

## 1. What it's built from

- **Spec:** Architecture v2 guide (27 Oct), PRD v3.3, and the YAML spec pack v0.3.0 vendored in the backend (requirements, event contract, Julia's comparison template, tutor policy).
- **Design:** the Figma file "Auramath", page "Screens · Prototype copy (27 Oct)": about 130 frames plus the component parts (Button, Option, Top bar and others).
- **Backend:** `auramath-backend`, branch `feature/initial-setup`, commit `4740765`. The app is wired to this version.
- **Decisions taken along the way:**
  - **Online-first:** a lesson needs the internet; it pauses when the connection drops and resumes where it stopped.
  - **Keys later:** Google and Apple keys come later, so sign-in is built and switches on when they arrive.
  - **Scope:** English only; iPhone first, with screens that adapt to iPad.

## 2. What's developed

**The child's lesson** (the core):
- The comparison word problem, end to end: hear the question, draw the bars, choose the sum, type the answers.
- **Help:** hints, "Show me how" (a worked example), and an interactive side lesson on hundreds, tens and ones.
- **"I know it":** shortcuts, with checks on the answer.
- **Mistakes:** wrong moves wobble, and "Help me" nudges after two misses.
- **Badges and wellbeing:** badges, the 30-minute daily cap, taking a break.
- **Online-first:** the server opens each lesson, every tap is sent live, the lesson pauses when the internet drops, and resume works across phones.
- **Grown-up's settings:** "Time each day" and "Read aloud" apply in the lesson, as does the bigger number pad.
- **Voice:** if the phone's voice fails, the child is told once and the words show big.

**Grown-up setup and sign-in:**
- Welcome → child's name, age, year, what they can do, goal → privacy and consent → sign in.
- Sign-in options: email link, Apple, Google.
- Ready → hand the phone over (with a passcode or Face ID lock), or give the child their own phone with a 6-digit code.

**The child's own phone:**
- "Who's here?" → type the code → "Is this you?" → joined.
- Wrong code, too many tries, and "this phone was removed".

**The grown-up area** (behind the passcode):
- Home, settings, family and phones, invite a grown-up, add a child.
- New pairing code, remove a phone.
- The book, the plan, the report and the month view.
- Report-email setting, data and rights (export, delete the account), sign out, forgot passcode.

**The child's screens around the lesson:** Home, Welcome back, My badges, badge close-up, My trail, the top of the trail, and the after-case screens (Case closed, On a roll, New skill, Sent to your grown-up).

**Quality:**
- All 311 automated tests pass.
- Every screen is rendered at iPhone 15, SE, Pro Max and iPad sizes. The test fails if anything overflows or misses a touch-target, label or contrast check.
- **End-to-end tests against the real backend:**
  1. A grown-up sets up a family and a lesson reaches the server.
  2. A child's phone pairs and its lesson reaches the server.
  - Both pass on the iPhone 16 simulator and the Android emulator.
